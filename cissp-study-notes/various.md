# Various

### Security Frameworks

Security frameworks are structured sets of guidelines, best practices, and standards used to implement and manage information security controls.

* NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)
* ISO/IEC 27001

### Control catalogs

* NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5
* ISO/IEC 27002
* CIS Controls

### Risk Frameworks

Risk frameworks provide methodologies and processes for identifying, assessing, treating, and monitoring risk.

* NIST SP 800-37 (RMF: Risk Management Framework)
* ISO/IEC 31000 (enterprise risk)
* OCTAVE
* FAIR

### Enterprise Security Architecture

Enterprise security architectures are blueprints or models that integrate security into an organization’s overall enterprise architecture.

* SABSA
* TOGAF
* Zachman Framework



### Enterprise Governance

Enterprise governance is the system for directing and controlling the whole organization—setting strategy, risk appetite, decision rights, and accountability—to create value and protect stakeholders. It aligns business and IT, mandates policies and controls, and provides oversight and assurance

* COBIT
* COSO\
